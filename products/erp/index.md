# Manorrock ERP: Your Business, Organized

Take control of your business operations—Manorrock ERP provides the essential tools to manage orders, payments, and more, starting with our Free Edition.

## Who is Manorrock ERP For?

Manorrock ERP is designed for:

- Small businesses and entrepreneurs needing simple order and payment management
- Hobbyists and makers who want to track sales and fulfillment
- Community groups, clubs, or non-profits needing basic ERP features
- Anyone who wants a modern, open, and developer-friendly ERP platform without vendor lock-in
