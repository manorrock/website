# Manorrock ERP Free

Manorrock ERP Free is a modern, zero-cost ERP solution designed for small businesses, hobbyists, and anyone who needs to manage orders and payments without the complexity or cost of traditional ERP systems.

## Key Features
- Simple setup – get started in minutes
- Modern, intuitive UI
- Production-ready out of the box

## Get Started
[View on GitHub](https://github.com/manorrock/erp)

## Why Choose Manorrock ERP Free?

- **Zero cost, zero hassle:** Start managing your business instantly with no fees or hidden charges.
- **Modern, mobile-ready interface:** Manage orders and payments easily.
- **Effortless order management:** Enter, track, and fulfill orders with ease.
- **Simple payment tracking:** Stay on top of unpaid orders and payment status.
